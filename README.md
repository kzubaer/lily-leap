# Lily Leap

A frog-and-snake pond game: hop across lily pads, catch flies with your tongue, and stay one leap ahead of the snake.

**Play:** https://kzubaer.github.io/lily-leap/

- Two modes: **Classic** and **Sinking pads**
- Lily pads drift in and out during play, so the pond keeps changing
- Easy, Normal and Hard
- Power-ups: speed boost, shield, snake freeze and long tongue
- Achievements, lives and a best score for each mode and difficulty
- Touch, mouse or keyboard (arrow keys or WASD to hop, Space to catch, P to pause, R to restart while paused)

On iPhone or iPad, open the link in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen** to get an app icon. The game then opens full screen and works offline.

Fonts: Lilita One and Nunito, under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/`).
