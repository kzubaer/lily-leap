Frog & Fang Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026. Developer: Kazi Md Zubaer.

Frog & Fang is a single-player game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we don't collect any personal information. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, your scores and achievements are shared with that service.

Information we collect

None. Frog & Fang does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no accounts of its own. The only sign-in is the optional Google Play Games or Game Center sign-in described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your device so they are there next time you play:

  • your best score for each game mode and difficulty
  • the game mode and difficulty you last chose
  • whether sound is on or off
  • which achievements you've earned, and your progress towards them (for example, total flies caught)

This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. The one exception: if you sign in to Google Play Games or Game Center, your best scores and earned achievements are sent to Google or Apple as described below. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes what is stored on your device.

Online leaderboards and achievements (optional)

The app offers online leaderboards and achievements through Google Play Games (on Android) and Game Center (on Apple devices). They are optional, and you can play without signing in.

If you sign in, the app sends your scores and earned achievements to Google or Apple. Your game profile, such as your gamer name or nickname, your scores and your achievements may be shown to other players. Google and Apple handle this information under their own privacy policies and account settings, which also control who can see your profile. As the developer, we can see leaderboard entries (player names and scores) in Google's and Apple's developer tools. We use this only to run the leaderboards, for example to remove cheated scores. We do not receive your email address or other account details.

Network access, advertising and analytics

The game runs on your device. It connects to the internet only to send scores and achievements to, and show leaderboards and achievements from, Google Play Games or Game Center, and only if you sign in. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases.

Permissions

Frog & Fang does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Signing in to Google Play Games or Game Center is subject to Google's and Apple's own account rules, including their age requirements.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
